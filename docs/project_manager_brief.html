1. Executive Summary & Product Architecture

Vyora is a production-grade multi-tenant GST billing, receivables, supplier payables, and traceable inventory management platform for Indian small-to-medium businesses (retailers, wholesalers, manufacturing, and distributors).

vyora-backend NestJS 11 + Mongoose 8

Decoupled modular REST service. Multi-tenant boundary isolation enforced via TenantGuard, RolesGuard, and PlanGuard. Minor currency integer precision across all ledgers.

vyora-frontend React 19 + Vite 6 + Tailwind 4

Mobile-first administrative dashboard using Theme B — Teal Ledger design system. Dynamic feature gating, responsive tables with horizontal scroll safeguards, and real-time role switchboard.

vyora-website Next.js Marketing & Blog

Public marketing site at D:\project\vyora-website featuring product capabilities, pricing plans (Starter, Growth, Business Pro), and SEO content blog.

vyora-mobile Planned React Native

Upcoming cross-platform mobile POS & counter invoicing app consuming the existing authenticated backend API.

2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) System

The platform strictly separates authority between business owners and operational staff to protect financials, catalog integrity, and audit compliance.

User Role Permission Level Permitted Capabilities Restrictions & Safeguards
OWNER / ADMIN Full Management Control • Create, Edit, Finalize, and Cancel Invoices
• Add and Archive Products & Catalog Items
• Add and Edit Customers & Suppliers
• Record Purchases & Payment Vouchers
• Adjust Stock (Business Pro Plan)
• Switch user roles and toggle active status
• Upgrade or change subscription plans 		• Cannot demote or suspend the last remaining owner in the business.
• Audit trail logs identity for all modifications.
STAFF View-Only Operational • View Dashboard revenue metrics & stats
• View & Search Invoices, preview & print PDFs

• Browse Customer & Supplier ledgers

• Backend RolesGuard rejects mutation requests with 403 Forbidden.

3. Dedicated Team & Roles Management (`/roles`)

A centralized management screen allowing business owners to monitor staff accounts, invite team members, and toggle roles instantly with live database synchronization.

  • Interactive Role Switch: Each team member has a dedicated switch toggle. Switching ON grants OWNER (Full Access); switching OFF sets STAFF (View Only). Changes apply immediately via PATCH /business/staff/:id/role.
  • Access Status Toggle: An active status switch allows owners to temporarily suspend or re-activate member access via PATCH /business/staff/:id/status without deleting their records.
  • Protective Guardrails: The last remaining owner is protected by backend invariants preventing demotion or suspension, ensuring the business is never locked out.
  • Member Invitation Modal: Owners can invite new staff members with required fields marked with prominent red asterisks (*). System sets standard initial credentials for frictionless onboarding.
